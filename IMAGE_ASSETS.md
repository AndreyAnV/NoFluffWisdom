# Image Asset Suggestions — NoFluffWisdom Landing Page

No images were generated per the brief. Below is every placeholder in the code, what it needs, and a prompt idea for sourcing or generating it later.

---

### 1. `<!-- HERO BACKGROUND IMAGE -->`
- **Location:** `.hero__bg`, behind the headline
- **Purpose:** Atmospheric depth behind the hero without competing with the headline
- **Style:** Extremely subtle — near-black textural photography (paper grain, ink, or soft desk-top light), NOT a literal photo of a person or screen. Should read almost as texture, not subject.
- **Dimensions:** 2400×1600px min, optimized for full-bleed background, low-contrast
- **Prompt idea:** *"Macro photograph of dark olive-green textured paper under a single soft directional light, minimal grain, almost abstract, muted, editorial — no text, no people"*

### 2. `<!-- FEATURE IMAGE -->` (×4, one per content card)
- **Location:** `.content-card__media`
- **Purpose:** Give each framework/guide/playbook a distinct visual identity without stock-photo clichés
- **Style:** Abstract editorial photography or fine linework illustration — objects/materials associated with disciplined thinking (a single pen and notebook, an hourglass, a stack of index cards, a minimal desk setup). Consistent muted olive/platinum/dark-red toning across all four for cohesion.
- **Dimensions:** 1200×750px (16:10) for standard cards; 1200×1500px for the large flagship card
- **Prompt idea:** *"Minimalist still-life photograph of a single fountain pen resting on cream paper, dark olive-green background, soft studio lighting, editorial magazine style, muted tones"*

### 3. `<!-- TESTIMONIAL PORTRAIT -->` (×3)
- **Location:** `.testimonial__portrait`
- **Purpose:** Humanize the testimonials with real reader photos
- **Style:** Simple circular headshots, neutral background, natural light, consistent crop and color grade across all three
- **Dimensions:** 200×200px (1:1, will be masked to a circle)
- **Note:** Should be real reader photos with permission once available — do not use AI-generated faces for testimonials, as this can undermine credibility if discovered.

### 4. `<!-- ICON -->` (×4, Experience section)
- **Location:** `.experience__icon`
- **Purpose:** Quick visual anchor for each "what you get" item (weekly email, framework library, no sponsors, quieter inbox)
- **Style:** Single-weight line icons (1.5px stroke), no fill, matching the page's disciplined/minimal tone — avoid playful or rounded icon sets
- **Dimensions:** 24×24px SVG, rendered inside a 44×44px container
- **Suggested set:** an envelope/mail-open icon, a stacked-books or archive icon, a no-symbol/shield icon, an inbox icon — sourced from a consistent line-icon family (e.g. Phosphor, Lucide) rather than mixed sets

---

**General guidance:** Keep every asset within the Black Olive / Platinum / Dark Red palette — no stock-photo blues, no bright saturated tones. If in doubt, desaturate and shift toward olive.
